PART I

Item 1. Business

Acme Industries designs, manufactures and services industrial automation equipment for discrete and process manufacturing customers. The Company operates through three reportable segments: Automation Systems, Industrial Services, and Digital Solutions. As of December 31, 2023, the Company employed approximately 41,200 people across 27 countries.

The Automation Systems segment supplies programmable controllers, motion systems and safety equipment to original equipment manufacturers and end users. Industrial Services provides installation, maintenance and lifecycle support under multi-year contracts. Digital Solutions comprises the Company's software subscription business, including the Acme Connect asset-monitoring platform.

No single customer accounted for more than 10% of consolidated net sales in fiscal 2023, 2022 or 2021.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Demand for the Company's products is cyclical and closely correlated with capital expenditure budgets in the automotive, semiconductor and consumer packaged goods industries. A downturn in any of these end markets has in the past reduced, and may in the future reduce, order volumes and operating margins.

The Company sources certain semiconductor components from a limited number of suppliers, several of which are located in a single geographic region. Interruption of supply from these vendors has previously extended lead times and could in the future delay revenue recognition on committed orders.

Approximately 58% of consolidated net sales in fiscal 2023 were denominated in currencies other than the U.S. dollar. A strengthening dollar reduces reported revenue and operating income on translation.

The Company is subject to evolving climate disclosure requirements in the European Union and California. Compliance may require additional data collection systems and could increase operating expenses.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Consolidated net sales for fiscal 2023 increased over fiscal 2022, driven by price realization in Automation Systems and continued subscription growth in Digital Solutions, partially offset by lower project volumes in Industrial Services. Gross margin expanded as supply chain costs normalized from the elevated levels experienced in fiscal 2022.

Operating expenses increased primarily as a result of higher research and development spending on the Acme Connect platform and incremental selling costs associated with the Company's expansion in Southeast Asia.
